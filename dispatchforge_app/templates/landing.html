Product preview

A clear view of what Elveriq does today.

Elveriq is under active development. The public sample uses fictional records and is intended for evaluation, not business critical use.

Who is Elveriq being built for?

Independent warehouse operators, small distributors, wholesalers, and teams that need a clear record of stock and warehouse activity.

What can I explore in the live sample?

The sample workspace covers product inventory, supplier purchasing, receiving, order fulfillment, stock transfers, returns, and operational reporting.

Does the public sample use real warehouse data?

No. The sample uses fictional information. It is designed to show application workflows without exposing customer records.

Can Elveriq manage multiple warehouse locations?

The application models inventory by warehouse location and includes internal stock transfer workflows. We are continuing to validate reliability and deployment behavior.

Is Elveriq ready for business critical inventory?

Not yet. The hosted experience is an early software preview. Production readiness requires additional operational testing, security review, backup procedures, and deployment validation.

What does it cost to explore the sample?

The public sample is available without entering payment information. Commercial pricing has not been announced.
